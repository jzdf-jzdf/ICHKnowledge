# Rationale and Design of a Randomized, Double-Blind Trial Evaluating the Efficacy of Tranexamic Acid on Hematoma Expansion and Peri-hematomal Edema in Patients with Spontaneous Intracerebral Hemorrhage within $\mathbf{4.56}$ after Symptom Onset: The THE-ICH Trial Protocol  

Chao Jiang, MD, PhD,\* Jiarui Wang,† Jian Wang, MD, PhD,\* ‡ and Jiewen Zhang, MD, PhD§, on behalf of the THE-ICH investigator  

Background: Hematoma expansion (HE) and peri-hematomal edema (PHE) are associated with adverse outcomes of patients with acute spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage (sICH). Due to a lack of proven treatments, it is critical to explore novel treatments for HE and PHE to improve functional recovery after sICH. Methods: This is a prospective, multicenter, placebo-controlled, double-blind, and randomized clinical study of approximately 2400 patients with sICH. Patients within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ of sICH onset that fulfilling the clinical criteria for diagnosis (e.g. age more than 18 years old, the Glasgow Coma Scal ${}>7$ , and no planned surgery) will randomly receive either intravenous tranexamic acid (TXA) $1\mathrm{~g~}10\cdot$ -min bolus followed by $^\textrm{\scriptsize1g}$ eight-hour infusion or placebo (sodium chloride $0.9\%$ ). Clinical data including the ICH score and the Glasgow Coma Scale score will be collected on admission. After assessment of HE and PHE expansion, follow-up will be conducted with enrolled patients for 90 days. Results: Primary outcome metrics are HE (defined as either ${>}33\%$ or $>6~\mathrm{ml}$ increase from baseline) and PHE expansion rate at $24\pm3\mathrm{h}$ and $72\pm31$ h post-sICH. Secondary outcome metrics include mortality and the modified Rankin Scale on day 90 after sICH. Appropriate statistic methods will be used to evaluate the efficacy of TXA on patients with sICH within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ of symptom onset. Conclusions: HE usually occurs within the first few hours after onset of symptoms. It is essential to evaluate the efficacy of TXA on HE within a narrow window of time. This will be the first trial to evaluate the efficacy of TXA on HE and PHE expansion in sICH patients within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ after symptom onset. This trial is registered as ChiCTR1900027065 at http://www.chictr.org.cn.

# Introduction and rationale  

Acute spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage (sICH) is a devastating neurological injury of global importance, with a poor prognosis and few proven treatments.1,2 Although ICH only accounts for $20-30\%$ of all stroke cases globally, the disease incidence and burden of ICH are higher in many Asian and lower-income countries.3-5 Mass effect induced by hematoma formation and expansion, and perihematomal edema (PHE) triggered by iron toxicity and inflammatory response result in both immediate and delayed neurological impairment after sICH.2,5 Treatment of acute sICH remains challenging,6 and it is urgent to explore novel approaches that can inhibit HE and PHE expansion and improve functional recovery after sICH.  

HE occurs predominantly during the $24\mathrm{~h~}$ after sICH onset; but delayed hematoma growth can occur, particularly in anticoagulated patients.7,8 HE was defined as either ${>}33\%$ or ${>}6~\mathrm{ml}$ increase from baseline.8-11. Clinical studies have indicated that HE is an independent predictor of neurological outcomes in patients with acute sICH.12,13 The mechanisms underlying HE are not entirely clear. Risk factors identified for HE include blood pressure, and vascular and coagulation disorders.14  

Previous clinical trials have investigated the efficacy of blood pressure control or hemostatic therapy on HE and functional recovery after sICH.6,7 Lowering arterial blood pressure did not inhibit hematoma growth or reduce mortality after sICH.15 FAST trial also revealed that recombinant FVIIa did not show clear clinical benefits and increased thromboembolic risk in unselected ICH patients.6,16 TICH-2 trial showed that tranexamic acid (TXA) exhibited potential benefits with reductions in HE, early mortality, and thromboembolic risk, but it did not promote functional recovery on day 90 after sICH.11 It is essential to further develop an understanding of the efficacy of TXA on HE and neurological function in patients with sICH.  

Although the mechanisms that underlie HE are currently unclear, cerebral bleeding that continued will contribute to the occurrence of HE in the first few hours of sICH. Animal studies have revealed that cerebral bleeding lasts for a few hours after the onset of sICH.17-19. Clinical studies have also suggested that HE mostly occurs in the first few hours after sICH onset.8,20 In addition, hematoma volume appears to be a predictor of PHE expansion, and PHE expansion also contributes to the early neurological deterioration of sICH.21,22 It has been previously illustrated that PHE increases by approximately $75\%$ in the first $^{24\mathrm{h,}}$ peaking around 5 6 days after sICH.4 Hence, strategies aimed at alleviating cerebral edema may also enhance the outcomes of patients with acute sICH. TXA has primarily been used for hemostatic therapy of some bleeding disorders.11 Studies also revealed that TXA could inhibit vascular permeability and alleviate tissue edema after bleedin g.23,24 The TICH-2 trial has shown the potential therapeutic value of TXA for patients with acute sICH.11 It is worth further exploring the therapeutic efficacy of TXA for HE, PHE expansion, and neurological function with a well designed randomised trial.  

Both the time window for use of hemostatic drugs and criteria for the selection of patients are two important factors to evaluate the efficacy of hemostatic drugs on HE and functional outcomes of patients with acute sICH. TICH-2 and other ongoing trials were carried out with the administration of TXA within 8 hours after sICH.11,25 It is necessary to evaluate the efficacy of TXA on HE and functional outcomes of patients with acute sICH within a narrow time window. Moreover, we observed that patients with acute sICH received surgical treatments were not excluded from the TICH-2 trial.11 However, the TICH-2 trial did not analyze the influence of surgical methods used and time from symptom onset to surgical treatments on the outcomes of patients with acute sICH.11 Studies have revealed that the duration of time between symptom onset and the beginning of surgical treatment, as well as the specific surgical techniques used, might have profound influence on the functional outcomes of patients with acute sICH. The efficacy of TXA on sICH patients without surgical plan may also warrant further verification. The STOP-AUST Trial is observing the effect of TXA administered within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ after symptom onset on HE predicted by spot sign in sICH patients without surgical plan.26 However, the spot sign could not predict all instances of HE after acute sICH. In addition, the mechanism for the formation of spot sign is currently unknown,27,28 thus it is unclear whether TXA is suitable to be used. Additional studies on the limitations of HE prediction with spot sign may be needed. The THEICH Trial will evaluate the efficacy of TXA administered within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ after symptom onset on HE, PHE expansion, and neurological function in sICH patients without surgical plan.  



# Methods  



# Design  

THE-ICH (Registered number: ChiCTR1900027065) is a prospective, multicenter, placebo-controlled, doubleblind, investigator-led, and randomized clinical trial. The primary efficacy objective is to determine whether TXA is superior to placebo in subjects with acute sICH. This study includes a screening phase, a double-blind treatment phase and a follow-up phase (Fig. 1). Patients with acute sICH fulfilling clinical criteria will receive either intravenous TXA $1\textrm{g}10$ -min bolus followed by ${1\mathrm{g}}$ eighthour infusion or placebo (sodium chloride $0.9\%$ ) within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ after sICH onset. All patients will receive computed tomography (CT) scan on admission. Additional CT scans will be performed at $24\pm3\mathrm{h}$ and $72\pm3\mathrm{h}$ to evaluate HE and PHE expansion after sICH. Follow up will be conducted for 90 days to observe the efficacy of TXA on mortality and recovery of neurological function. The safety of TXA will also be monitored in this trial.  



# Patient population, inclusion and exclusion criteria  

The efficacy of tranexamic acid on hematoma expansion and peri-hematomal edema in patients with spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage (THE-ICH Trial) will prospectively include an estimated 2400 participants in multiple clinical centers of Henan province. Participants fulfilling the eligibility will be randomized (1:1) to receive either TXA or placebo $0.9\%$ saline) within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ after sICH onset. The inclusion and exclusion criteria of this clinical trial are summarized in Table 1. All patients will undergo three head CT scans on admission, at $24\pm3\mathrm{h},$ and $72\pm3\mathrm{h}$ after symptom onset respectively. Lesion volume, ICH score, and GCS and NIHSS score will be used to evaluate the severity of cerebral injury on admission. The laboratory test results, demographic variables, and stroke-related comorbidities will be collected from all patients on admission. HE and PHE expansion will be calculated at $24\pm3\mathrm{h}$ and $72\pm3\mathrm{h}$ after symptom onset. Mortality, and GCS and NIHSS score will be documented on day 7 after symptom onset or at early discharge. Mortality and mRS will be used to evaluate the therapeutic efficacy of TXA for sICH on day 90 after symptom onset. We will also assess the safety of TXA for the treatment of sICH by monitoring the occurrence of ischemic events and organ dysfunction.

# Randomization  

Random number tables were used to randomize patients fulfilling the criteria into 2 study groups. The random numbers were generated with Stata12.0 software in a secure internet center. Randomization is stratified by branch centers (to avoid the influence of small site on the allocation concealment, more than 100 patients will be enrolled in each branch center). The researcher who generated the random numbers would not be permitted to participate in the following clinical study. We will define block length as 4 when we program the randomization with Stata12.0 software using the following protocols. 86359427 will be used as random seed number. The sample size in each center must be an integral multiple of block.4 The random numbers (1 or 2) only can be known (via scratch cards) by the clinical investigators after the enrollment of each patient meeting the study criteria.  



# Clinical data collection and blindness  

Clinical covariables including age, gender, previous medical history, dyslipidemia, and medication use will be collected by the investigators using a case report form (CRF) at each research center. Immediately after patients are admitted, the following variables will be collected prospectively: systolic blood pressure, National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS), GCS, ICH score, and time of symptom onset. The elapsed time from symptom onset to randomization will also be recorded. Outcomes at 90 days after sICH onset will be assessed blindly by trained study staff via telephone interview with the modified Rankin Scale (mRS). We also will collect imaging data using a standardized data collection form. Imaging data including hematoma expansion and PHE expansion volume measured from the CT scan will be blindly evaluated by a senior neurologist.

# Treatment  

Patients randomized to TXA will receive intravenous TXA 1 g 10-min bolus followed by ${1\mathrm{g}}$ eight-hour infusion within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ after symptom onset. Patients randomized to placebo will receive intravenous sodium chloride $0.9\%$ at the same time points as patients in the TXA group.  

All patients will receive medical management according to the guidelines of the American Heart Association/ American Stroke Association Stroke Council for the treatment of spontaneous ICH in adults, including treatments for the control of blood pressure, cerebral edema, and glycemia; gastric protection; nutritional support; and the prevention of complications.  

The use of neuroprotective agents and traditional Chinese medicine is prohibited in this study. Patients with hypertension and/or diabetes mellitus will receive appropriate treatments. Patients at risk for ulcerative gastrointestinal disease or gastrointestinal bleeding can receive prophylactic treatment with proton pump inhibitors at the investigator s discretion. Patients with complications from infections or elevation of intracranial pressure should receive antibiotic therapy or dehydration therapy. These medications will be recorded as concomitant medications on the electronic CRF.  



# Primary outcomes  

The primary metrics for outcome are HE and PHE expansion rate at $24\pm3\mathrm{~h~}$ and $72\pm3\mathrm{~h~}$ post sICH. HE will be calculated blindly using the ABC/2 method from axial CT images at multiple time points by a senior neurologist.2,13 HE was defined as either a ${>}33\%$ or ${>}6~\mathrm{ml}$ increase from baseline.8-11. Previously established methods will be used to evaluate PHE expansion rate at $24\pm$ $^{3\mathrm{{h}}}$ and $72\pm3\mathrm{h}$ with Medical Image Processing, Analysis, and Visualization (MIPAV) software after sICH.12,22,29 PHE expansion rate will be calculated by using the formula (PHE at each time point- PHE at baseline)/(Time to CT scan at each time point - Time to baseline CT scan, in hours) and will be quantified as mL/hour.  



# Secondary outcomes  

Secondary outcome measures include mortality and NIHSS on day 7, and mortality and mRS on day 90 after sICH. As previously described, the $\mathrm{NIHSS}^{30}$ will be used to evaluate the severity of neurological deficits on day 7 after sICH. We also will use the modified mRS, a 7-point scale ranging from 0 (no symptoms) to 6 (death),31,32 to assess neurological outcomes on day 90 after symptom onset. To satisfy the proportional odds assumption, we reclassified the mRS as follows: mRS 02 (good outcome), mRS 36 (poor outcome).31,32  



# Safety evaluations  

An independent data monitoring committee (IDMC) will monitor data on an ongoing basis to ensure the continuing safety of the subjects enrolled in this study and to meet efficacy objectives. Mortality, venous thromboembolism, vascular occlusive events (stroke/transient ischemic attack/myocardial infarction/peripheral artery disease), and seizures will be monitored until day 90 after sICH. In addition, organ function including liver and renal functions, and blood coagulation will also be monitored until day 90 after sICH.  



# Data monitoring  

Data management and quality control will be monitored by IDMC in the following three stages: project design stage, research implementation stage, and data arrangement stage. The data management committee will assess the efficacy and safety of the data every three months to decide whether this trial should be prematurely terminated or continued. For the analysis of the results, the following data will be excluded: (1) Patients lost on follow-up. (2) Patients withdrawn for the concurrence of other diseases that will influence their capability on follow-up, (3) Data determined to be unreasonable by the Data Management Committee.  



# Sample size estimates and statistical analyses  

Sample sizes were determined with power analysis based on previous studies (with a type I error of 0.05, power of 0.8).11,33 SPSS 13.0 (SPSS Inc., Chicago, IL, USA) or SAS software version 9.4 will be used for the statistical analysis of this study. Data will be presented as mean (SEM) or median (range) for continuous variables and percentage (count) for categorical variables, unless otherwise indicated. $P{<}0.05$ was considered statistically significant. The Student's t-test, Chi-squared test, or Fisher exact test will be used as appropriate for univariate analysis of this study. All tests of significance will be two-tailed. The distribution of data will be determined with the KolmogorovSmirnov test. If the data do not have a normal distribution, appropriate transformation will be used for the analysis of these data. Univariable analysis for variables associated with outcomes on day 90 will be performed with appropriate tests. All associations significant at $P{<}0.10$ will be permitted to be entered into a binary logistic model or ordinal logistic regression. If possible, the confounding factors should be adjusted when we are analyzing the data. After the finish of this study, the raw data will be submitted to http://www.meadresman.org.cn.

# Discussion  

Stroke is a leading cause of death and serious long-term disability.34 With recent advances, mortality from ischemic stroke has decreased over the past decade.34 Following the development of intravenous thrombolysis as a successful treatment for ischemic stroke, advances in endovascular therapy have also contributed to the reduction in the mortality rates of ischemic stroke.35 Due to limitations in the selection of therapeutic intervention, the prognosis of sICH is currently worse than ischemic stroke.36 THE-ICH will be designed to explore whether TXA administrated within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ can arrest HE and PHE expansion and improve neurological recovery after acute sICH.  

Previous clinical trials have tried to look for effective interventions for the arrest of HE with blood pressure control and hemostatic therapy after sICH.6,14,36 However these trials did not produce satisfactory results, for they found that rapidly lowering blood pressure, hemostatic therapy with platelet transfusion, or recombinant factor VIIa might only suitable for the treatment of a small number of specific sICH patients.6,7,37 With features of low cost, lessened side effects, and ease of access and administration, TXA has attracted the attention of neurologists for its ability to inhibit the activation of plasminogen, and can be given to reverse thrombolysis in the setting of hemorrhage.11,25,26 Although previous studies have investigated the efficacy of TXA on sICH, the clinical effectiveness of TXA has not been shown.11,38,39  

Hematoma volume may predict PHE expansion after sICH.22 In addition, TXA also has been demonstrated to have an ability to reduce tissue edema.23,24 THE-ICH will assess the efficacy of TXA on HE, PHE expansion and the prognosis of patients with acute sICH. To eliminate the bias induced by surgical treatments, patients with surgical plan will be excluded from THE-ICH trial. However, our study is limited because patients will only originate from one region (Henan province). Moreover, TXA administrated within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ in THE-ICH may influence the hemostatic efficacy of TXA, for $73\%$ cerebral bleeding will last in the first $^{3\textrm{h}}$ after symptom onset of sICH.8,20 It will be difficult to recruit so many patients as listed in this protocol within $^{3\textrm{h}}$ after symptom onset of sICH. We will try our best to recruit sICH patients within $^{3\mathrm{h}}$ after symptom onset, which would permit us to perform subgroup analysis for these patients who received TXA therapy within $^{3\mathrm{h}}$ after symptom onset in the further.  



# Summary and conclusion  

To our knowledge, this study will be the first doubleblind, placebo-controlled, and randomized clinical trial designed to provide statistically robust results that explore the role of TXA used within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ after symptom onset on HE, PHE expansion and prognosis in patients with acute sICH. If this clinical trial can be completed successfully, it may provide some useful basis as to novel therapies for acute sICH.
