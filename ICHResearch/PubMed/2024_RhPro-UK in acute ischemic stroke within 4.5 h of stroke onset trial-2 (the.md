# RhPro-UK in acute ischemic stroke within 4.5 h of stroke onset trial-2 (the PROST-2 study): Rationale and design of a multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label, blinded-endpoint, controlled phase 3 non-inferiority trial  

Shuya Li1 , Hong-Qiu $\mathbf{Gu}^{\mathsf{I}}(\widehat{\mathbb{D}})$ , Baoyu Feng1, Qiang Dong2, Dongsheng Fan3, Yun $\times\mathbf{u}^{4}\textcircled{\textcircled{\scriptsize{10}}}$ , Suiqiang Zhu5 and Yongjun Wang1 iD  

# Abstract  

Background: Recombinant prourokinase (rhPro-UK) is a specific plasmin activator, which has been approved to treat acute myocardial infarction in China.  

Aim: This phase 3 trial aimed to further demonstrate the efficacy and safety of rhPro-UK in patients with acute ischemic stroke (AIS) within $4.5~\mathrm{h}$ of symptom onset.  

Methods and design: RhPro-UK in AIS within $4.5~\mathrm{h}$ of stroke onset trial-2 (PROST-2) is a multicenter, prospective randomized, open-label, blinded end-point, non-inferiority, recombinant tissue plasmin activator (rt-PA)-controlled, phase 3 trial. A total of 1552 patients who are eligible for intravenous thrombolytic therapy from 72 clinical sites will be randomly assigned to receive either rhPro-UK $35\mathsf{mg}$ (15 mg bolus $^+$ 20 mg infusion/30 min) or rt-PA $0.9\mathrm{mg/kg}$ ( $10\%$ bolus + $90\%$ infusion/1 h).  

Study Outcomes: The primary outcome is the proportion of patients with a modified Rankin Scale $(\mathsf{mRS})$ score of 0–1 at 90 days. Secondary efficacy outcomes include the proportion of patients with $\mathsf{mRS}$ score of 0–2, the distribution of mRS, self-care ability in daily life on the Barthel Index at 90 days, the proportion of subjects with $\geq4$ points decrease in National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS) score or NIHSS score $\leqslant|$ from baseline at $24~\mathsf{h}$ and 7 days after treatment. Safety outcomes are symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) and major systematic bleeding within 7 days as well as death from all causes within 90 days.  

Discussion: The results from the PROST-2 trial will comprehensively elucidate the efficacy and safety profile of rhProUK as a potential alternative agent for stroke thrombolysis.

# Introduction  

# Study organization  

Recombinant tissue plasmin activator (rt-PA) represents an evidence-based thrombolytic agent for intravenous thrombolysis (IVT) in patients with acute ischemic stroke (AIS).1 The global shortage of rt-PA calls for immediate action to ensure the utilization of the most appropriate thrombolytic agents in patients with ischemic stroke.2–4  

Recombinant prourokinase (rhPro-UK), which has the same amino acid sequence as natural prourokinase, has been proven effective and safe for patients with acute myocardial infarction.5–7 The single-chain proenzyme precursor of urokinase, consisting of 411 amino acids and glycosylated, is derived from transfected murine SP2/0 hybridoma cells. As a fibrin-specific plasmin activator, rhPro-UK will only bind and activate fibrin-combined plasmin, which conformation has been changed due to binding to the lysine residue of $\mathrm{Y/E}$ frgament, converting it into plasmin, resulting in a cascade amplification effect that achieves rapid and effective thrombolysis.8 Because the release of endogenous t-PA is triggered only by thromboembolic thrombus, rhProUK primarily targets fibrin within the embolic thrombus site and does not induce the dissolution of hemostatic thrombus.9,10 Therefore, the potential advantage of prourokinase lies in its ability to minimize systemic bleeding and intracranial hemorrhage.11 To investigate the safety and preliminary efficacy of rhPro-UK in patients with AIS, we have completed a phase 2 trial (Chinese Clinical Trial Registry Identifier: ChiCTR1800016519)12 and a sample size expanded explorative clinical trial (PROST trial, Clinical Trial Registration Identifier: NCT03541668).13 The results of these prior trials preliminarily suggested that, compared to rt-PA, IVT with $35\mathrm{mg}$ of rhPro-UK showed similar efficacy in patients within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ of AIS onset. No significant difference was observed regarding the incidence of symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) and mortality. The incidence of any systemic bleeding was significantly lower in the rhPro-UK group compared to the rt-PA group $25.8\%$ vs $42.2\%$ , risk difference: $-16.5\%$ , $95\%$ confidence interval (CI): $-23.6\%$ , $-9.4\%$ , $p{<}0.001\$ .  

The RhPro-UK in AIS within $4.5\mathrm{h}$ of stroke onset trial-2 (PROST-2) trial aims to further validate the efficacy and safety of rhPro-UK in the treatment of AIS within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ after stroke onset.  

Before the initiation of the clinical trial, an academic committee, a clinical event committee (CEC) and an independent data monitoring committee (DMC) have been established. The academic committee will evaluate the scientific feasibility of the clinical trial protocol before the start of the clinical trial. The CEC is initiated to independently determine the types of all-cause mortality events, sICH events within 7 days after treatment and their correlation with the administered drug. The DMC will hold DMC meetings as required, review and analyze all safety data, and advise the sponsor whether to continue, suspend, or stop the trial. The details of the academic committee, the CEC, and the DMC can be found in the Supplemental materials (Supplemental 1–3).  

# Patient population  

# Inclusion criteria  

1.	 Clinical diagnosis as AIS according to the Chinese Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Treatment of Acute Ischemic Stroke 2018;   
2.	 Age $\geqslant18$ years;   
3.	 National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS) score $\geq4$ and $\leqslant25$ ;   
4.	 Stroke symptoms onset time $\leqslant4.5\mathrm{h}$ . The onset time defined as the last time that the patient’s function is known to be normal;   
5.	 Clinical manifestations of stroke for at least $30\mathrm{min}$ and without significant improvement before treatment;   
6.	 Informed consent from the patients or their guardians.  

# Exclusion criteria

# Randomization and masking  

The eligible patients will be randomly assigned at a ratio of 1:1 to receive rhPro-UK or rt-PA by an interactive web response system (CLT Inc V1.0, China). The randomization will be stratified by the severity of neurological defect measured by the NIHSS score ( $\leqslant7$ points, 8–14 points, $\geqslant15$ points) at baseline. The thrombolytic treatments will be administered in an open-label manner. The authorized investigators, who have undergone standardized training, will conduct independent blind endpoint evaluations without being aware of patients grouping.  

# Treatment intervention  

The rhPro-UK Group: rhPro-UK $\mathrm{35mg(15mgbolus+20mg}$ infusion $/30\mathrm{min})$ ).  

The rt-PA Group: $0.9\mathrm{mg/kg}$ ( $10\%$ bolus $+90\%$ infusion/1 h).  

Both groups subsequently will receive treatment in accordance with established guidelines and clinical practice. See Figure 1 for the PROST-2 trial schedule of activities chart.  

# Outcomes  

Primary outcome.  Proportion of subjects with an mRS score of 0–1 point at 90 days after treatment.  

# Secondary outcomes  

1.	 Proportion of subjects with an mRS score of 0–2 points at 90 days after treatment;   
2.	 Distribution of mRS scores at 90 days after treatment;   
3. Proportion of subjects with $\geq4$ points decrease in NIHSS score (or NIHSS score $\leqslant1$ ) from baseline at $^{241}$ after treatment;   
4.	 Proportion of subjects with $\geq4$ points decrease in NIHSS score (or NIHSS score $\leqslant1$ ) from baseline at 7 days after treatment;   
5.	 Difference of NIHSS score at $^{241}$ after the treatment from the baseline;   
6.	 Difference of NIHSS score at 7 days after the treatment from the baseline;   
7.	 Proportion of subjects with Barthel Index of 95–100 points at 90 days after treatment.  

# Safety outcomes  

1.	 The sICH events as defined by Safe Implementation of Thrombolysis in Stroke-Monitoring Study (SITS-MOST);14   
2.	 The sICH events as defined by European Cooperative Acute Stroke Study III (ECASS III);15   
3.	 Any intracranial hemorrhage event within 7 days after treatment;   
4.	 Any hemorrhage event (as defined by International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis (ISTH))16 within 7 days after treatment;   
5.	 All-cause mortality within 7 days after treatment;   
6.	 All-cause mortality within 90 days after treatment.  

# Statistical considerations  

Sample size.  The sample size is estimated based on the primary efficacy endpoint, the proportion of subjects with an mRS score of 0–1 point 90 days after treatment. Based on the meta-analysis of previous studies,15,17 the lower limit of RR for efficacy of rt-PA to placebo is 1.15. If at least $50\%$ of the most conservative estimate of the efficacy is required, the non-inferiority margin would be 0.93. According to the results of the previous two clinical trials,12,13 the proportion of primary outcome of the rt-PA group is set as $63.07\%$ . Assuming an efficacy ratio of 1.04, a statistical power of $80\%$ , and a one-sided significance level of 0.025, a total of 1398 patients (699 for each group) will be needed. The final sample size was increased to 1552 to accommodate a maximum drop-out rate of $10\%$ . If the drop-out rate is between $3\%{\sim}5\%$ , the determined sample size would provide a statistical power of $82\%{\sim}83\%$ .  

# Statistical analysis  

The efficacy outcomes will be analyzed according to the intention-to-treat principle. The primary outcome will be assessed using Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel (CMH) $\chi^{2}$ test, stratified by the baseline NIHSS score $(\leqslant7,8-14,\equiv15\$ . The relative risk between groups and its $95\%$ confidence interval (CI) will be used for group comparisons. The CI method will be used to verify the non-inferiority of rhProUK to rt-PA in the treatment of AIS. If the lower bound of the $95\%$ CI of relative risk is greater than 0.93, the noninferiority would be declared. For dichotomous secondary efficacy endpoints, the Newcombe method will be used to estimate rate differences between groups. Continuous secondary efficacy endpoints will be estimated using Wilcoxon rank-sum test, $t$ –test, or analysis of covariance. Besides, sensitivity analysis for efficacy outcomes will be conducted according to the per protocol principle.

# Enrollment characteristics  

The total number of patients enrolled is 1552 from 72 clinical sites in China. The first patient was enrolled on 29 January 2023. The study is expected to complete in 2024.  

# Discussion  

The PROST-2 trial is a pivotal trial that will provide evidence for the efficacy and safety of rhPro-UK versus rt-PA in the treatment of AIS within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ after stroke onset.  

Based on the findings from a phase 2, dose-finding clinical trial,12 the PROST trial with a sample size of 663 and a noninferiority margin of $10\%$ was designed to preliminarily observe the efficacy and safety of rhPro-UK $35\mathrm{mg}$ in patients with AIS.13 The primary outcome was the proportion of a mRS score of 0 to 1 at 90 days, which occurred in 215 patients $(65.2\%)$ in the rhPro-UK group and 214 patients $(64.3\%)$ in the rt-PA group (risk difference, 0.89; $95.4\%$ CI, $-6.52$ to 8.29). The safety profile was similar with the rate of sICH ( $1.5\%$ vs $1.8\%$ , defined by SITSMOST) and all-cause death $4.2\%$ vs $2.7\%$ ). Notably, there was a significantly lower incidence of any systemic bleeding in the rhPro-UK group compared to the rt-PA group $(25.8\%$ vs $42.2\%$ ).  

The pre-clinical studies explained the mechanisms underlying the enhanced safety of rhPro-UK, which has the ability to directly activate plasma endogenous plasmin $({\mathrm{Pg}})$ bound to fibrin that has been partially degraded by t-PA beforehand, resulting in the formation of a ternary complex $\mathrm{^{\mathrm{{c}}}rhPro\mathrm{-\mathrm{{UK}+fibrin+\mathrm{{Pg}}}}}$ .” This complex generates abundant plasmin to rapidly dissolve the thrombus.18,19 Given that only local pressure surges and tissue hypoxia induced by embolic thrombus can stimulate the release of t-PA from local vascular endothelial cells, rhPro-UK specifically dissolve embolic thrombus without affecting hemostatic thrombus.9 The clinical studies, PROACT trial, and PROACT II trial,20,21 tested the safety and efficacy of intraarterial recombinant pro-urokinase in patients with AIS caused by middle cerebral artery occlusion. The high thrombolytic activity and a favorable safety profile of rhPro-UK might meet the needs of patients with large vessel occlusion who are ineligible or do not have access to endovascular thrombectomy.4  

# Limitations  

There are several limitations of the PROST-2 trial. First, the open-label design of this study may have risk of biases. To reduce the bias, measures that maintain the integrity and impartiality of the evaluation process will be implemented, including employing independent investigators and ensuring their unawareness of grouping information. Second, due to the narrow thrombolysis time window, there is no mandatory requirement for advanced imaging of perfusion lesions or vessel occlusion, which will result in a lack of information regarding reperfusion and recanalization on imaging studies.  

In conclusion, the result of the PROST-2 trial will comprehensively elucidate the efficacy and safety of rhPro-UK in AIS treatment. This study may provide an alternative option of thrombolytic agents for IVT management.
