# Naoxintong Capsule for Secondary Prevention of Ischemic Stroke: A Multicenter, Randomized, and Placebo-Controlled Trial  

YU Xiao-fei1, ZHU Xu-ying1, YUAN Can-xing2, WU Dan-hong3, ZHAO Yu-wu4, YANG Jia-jun5, WANG Chang-de6, WU Wei-wen7, LIU Xue-yuan8, LIU Zhen-guo9, NIE Zhi-yu10, DENG Ben-qiang11, BAO Huan12, LI Long-xuan13, WANG Chun-yan14, ZHANG Hong-zhi1, ZHANG Jing-si1, HUANG Ji-han15, GONG Fan1, WANG Ming-zhe1, GUO Yong-mei1, SUN Yan1, and CAI Ding-fang1  

ABSTRACT Objective: To examine whether the combination of Naoxintong Capsule with standard care could further reduce the recurrence of ischemic stroke without increasing the risk of severe bleeding. Methods: A total of 23 Chinese medical centers participated in this trial. Adult patients with a history of ischemic stroke were randomly assigned in a 1:1 ratio using a block design to receive either Naoxintong Capsule ( $1.2~{\mathfrak{g}}$ orally, twice a day) or placebo in addition to standard care. The primary endpoint was recurrence of ischemic stroke within 2 years. Secondary outcomes included myocardial infarction, death due to recurrent ischemic stroke, and allcause mortality. The safety of drugs was monitored. Results were analyzed using the intention-to-treat principle.  

Results: A total of 2,200 patients were enrolled from March 2015 toMarch 2016,of whom 143 and 158 in the Naoxintong and placebo groups were lost to follow-up, respectively. Compared with the placebo group, the recurrence rate of ischemic stroke within 2 years was significantly lower in the Naoxintong group $[6.5\%$ VS. $9.5\%$ ,hazard ratio (HR): 0.665, $95\%$ confidence interval (Cl): 0.492-0.899, $_{P=0.0081}$ . The two groups showed no significant differences in the secondary outcomes and safety, including rates of severe hemorrhage, cerebral hemorrhage and subarachnoid hemorrhage $(P>0.05)$ . Conclusion: The combination of Naoxintong Capsule with standard care reduced the 2-year stroke recurrence rate in patients with ischemic stroke without increasing the risk of severe hemorrhage in high-risk patients. (Trial registration No. NCT02334969) KEYWORDS ischemic stroke, Naoxintong Capsule, secondary prevention, randomized controlled trial, Chinese medicine

Among patients with cerebral infarction, the prevalence of hypertension can be higher than $70\%$ .(5,6) The risk of ischemic stroke increases by $25\%$ with every 1 mmol/L increase in the serum cholesterol level.(7) Moreover, $60\%$ to $70\%$ of ischemic stroke patients have abnormal glucose metabolism, including impaired fasting blood glucose, abnormal glucose tolerance, and diabetes mellitus.(8) Secondary prevention in patients with stroke is crucial to reduce the recurrence rate.  

The Perindopril Protection against Recurrent Stroke Study (PROGRESS) demonstrated that antihypertensive therapy is effective in reducing the recurrence of stroke.(9) The Stroke Prevention by Aggressive Reduction in Cholesterol Levels (SPARCL) trial in patients with non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke showed that using atorvastatin intensively for 5 years reduced the risk of ischemic stroke by $16\%$ .(10)  

Antiplatelet therapy is the cornerstone for secondary prevention of ischemic stroke. Aspirin is widely used as the first choice for secondary prevention of ischemic stroke.(11) Other antiplatelet agents, such as clopidogrel and ticagrelor, have also been recommended.(12,13) The Clopidogrel in High-risk Patients with Acute Non-disabling Cerebrovascular Events (CHANCE) and the Platelet-Oriented Inhibition in New TIA and Minor Ischemic Stroke (POINT) trials demonstrated that early intervention with aspirin in combination with clopidogrel can reduce the risk of stroke recurrence within 90 days.(14) A study of 5,170 patients with acute minor stroke or transient ischemic stroke found that combined antiplatelet therapy was more effective than aspirin alone, without increasing the risk of hemorrhage.(15)  

In Chinese medicine, ischemic stroke is thought to be the result of blood stasis. In China, Naoxintong Capsule (脑心通胶囊) is widely used based on such a theory in patients with ischemic stroke as well as coronary artery disease. The capsule is modified from Buyang Huanwu Decoction (补阳还五汤), which is an ancient prescription. Naoxintong Capsule is composed of Scutellaria baicalensis Georgi, Paeonia veitchii Lynch, Salvia miltiorrhiza Bunge, Angelicae Sinensis Radix, Ligusticum chuanxiong hort, Persicae Semen, Carthamus tinctorius L., Boswellia carteri, Commiphora myrrha, Kadsura interior, Achyranthis  

Bidentatae Radix, Cinnamomumcassia Presl, Morus alba L., Pheretima, Hirudo and Pandinus cavimanus. In addition to limiting reperfusion injury caused by inflammatory factors,i6) Naoxintong Capsule has been shown to regulate cytokines, vascular growth factors and induce angiogenesis,(17) decrease low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C),(18) inhibit the process of atherosclerosis and enhance plaque stability in apolipoprotein E deficient mice.19) In a previous study using warfarin as the control, Naoxintong Capsule in combination with aspirin significantly reduced the risk of severe bleeding without affecting the incidence of ischemic stroke.(20)  

We conducted a multicenter randomized controlled trial to evaluate whether Naoxintong Capsule, when combined with standard secondary preventions, can further reduce stroke recurrence and the risk of severe bleeding.  

# METHODS  

# Study Design  

Twenty-three hospitals in Shanghai participated in this trial (Appendix 1). A total of 2,200 patients were recruited from March 2015 to March 2016, and the last enrolled patient was followed up until March 2018. All patients provided written informed consents and received standard treatment for ischemic stroke before randomization. The trial has been registered at ClinicalTrials.gov with registry No. NCT02334969 and the protocol was approved by the Ethics Committee of Shuguang Hospital Affliated to Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (approval No. 2014-368- 64-01) as well as other 22 participating hospitals.  

# Diagnostic Criteria  

The abrupt onset of neurologic deficits such as speech disturbance, weakness on half of one's body, seizure, headache and conversion disorder is considered as the hallmark of the diagnostic criteria of ischemic stroke.(21) The type of ischemic stroke was determined based on the Trial of Org 10172 in Acute Stroke Treatment (TOAST) classification as large artery atherosclerotic, cardiac cerebral embolism, small-artery occlusion lacunar, stroke of other determined etiology or stroke of other undetermined etiology.(22)  

# Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria  

Adults (18–80 years) with ischemic stroke were eligible to participate. Patients were included only if at least 10 days had passed since the onset of their previous stroke, and if they were able to take oral medications.  

Patients were excluded if they had (1) a New York Heart Association (NYHA) functional classification of Ⅱ or higher;(23) (2) hepatic injury, defined as alanine transaminase $\geqslant2$ times the upper normal limit; (3) renal damage, defined as serum creatinine $>2\mathsf{mg}/\mathsf{dL}$ ; (4) malignant neoplasm; (5) blood platelet count $<100\times10^{9}/\mathrm{L}$ or history of gastrointestinal bleeding; (6) psychiatric disease; (7) pregnancy or lactation; and/or (8) ongoing participation in any other clinical trial. To ensure the reliability of the trial, all the participants involved in this trial were not allowed to take other activating blood circulation herbs except Naoxintong Capsule.  

# Randomization and Concealment  

Subjects were randomized in a 1:1 ratio using a block design (block size: 20) to receive either Naoxintong Capsule or placebo in addition to standard care. The randomization sequence was generated using a computer by a statistician not otherwise involved in the study. Concealment was conducted using opaque envelopes. Patients, physicians, and evaluators were blinded to treatment allocation.  

# Sample Size Estimation  

Sample size requirement was estimated based on: (1) alpha $_{=0.05}$ , and (2) power $(1-\mathsf{beta}){=}0.8$ , (3) $7\%$ recurrence within 2 years in the placebo group,(11) (4) reduction of 2-year recurrence rate to $4\%$ in the Naoxintong group.(24) The calculation yielded 906 patients per group. Assuming a drop-out rate of $16\%$ , 1,079 patients were needed per group. Actually 2,200 patients were recruited.  

# Treatment  

All patients in the study received standard care as stipulated in the "2014 Chinese Guidelines for Secondary Prevention of Ischemic Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack".(25) All the interventions are in line with the "Guidelines for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients with Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack" of the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association (AHA/ASA).(26) In addition to standard care, the Naoxintong Group was treated with Naoxintong Capsule (1.2 g orally, twice a day; lot Nos. 141291 and 150330, Shaanxi Buchang  

Pharmaceuticals, China) for 24 months, and the control group was intervened with placebo (consisting of brown granulated sugar, starch, and $5.37\%$ Naoxintong, manufactured by Shaanxi Buchang Pharmaceuticals) for 24 months.  

The 2014 Chinese guideline recommends aspirin $(75-150\mathrm{mg/d)}$ monotherapy and (or) clopidogrel $(75\mathrm{~mg/d})$ monotherapy in patients with noncardioembolic ischemic stroke. Regarding minor stroke [National Institute of Health Stroke Scale $(\mathsf{NIHSS}){<}4]$ , a combination of aspirin and clopidogrel is considered for initiation within $^{24\textrm{h}}$ ,(25) which are in line with the 2014 AHA/ASA guideline recommends.(26) Aspirin $(100\mathrm{mg/d)}$ or clopidogrel $(75{\mathrm{~mg/d}})$ monotherapy was used to reduce the risk of recurrent stroke in the current trial; a combination of aspirin and clopidogrel was initiated in patients with minor stroke within $^{24\textrm{h}}$ of onset. Patients with cardioembolic stroke received warfarin or a new oral anticoagulant (e.g., dabigatran, rivaroxaban, apixaban and edoxaban).(25) Target for blood pressure control was $140/90~\mathrm{mm}~\mathsf{Hg}$ in previously untreated patients with hypertension and those with known hypertension.(26) Preferred antihypertensives were calcium channel blockers, $\upbeta$ -receptor blockers or adrenoreceptor blockers. LD $\lrcornerC<2.6\mathrm{mmol/L}$ is the target for long-term statin therapy,(26) and the secondary prevention is more effective when LD $L{\cdot}C{\lesssim}1.8\mathrm{mmol/L}$ .(25) In this trial, a small dosage of statin as $20~\mathsf{mg}$ atorvastatin or $10~\mathsf{mg}$ rosuvastatin calcium was used. Lifestyle change and drug interventions are important for patients with abnormal glucose metabolism to reduce the recurrence of stroke. The recommended target of the percent of glycosylated hemoglobin A1c $(\mathsf{HbA1c\%})$ level is below $7\%$ .(25) A strict control of blood glucose to make the $\mathsf{HbA1c\%}$ level below $6.5\%$ revealed a significant reduction of vascular events.(26,27) Concerning patients with ischemic stroke, treatment of glucose and lipid metabolism disorders, hypertension and anti-platelet medicines are equally important in secondary prevention. Other medications with potential anti-platelet or anticoagulant action were not allowed during the study period.  

# Observation Index  

The primary outcome was recurrence of ischemic stroke within 2 years after randomization. Secondary outcomes included myocardial infarction, death due to recurrent ischemic stroke, and all-cause mortality.  

# Safety Monitoring  

Thestudydrugs ( NaoxintongCapsule or placebo) were discontinued in the events of (1) recurrence of ischemic stroke, (2) hemorrhagic stroke, (3) subarachnoid hemorrhage, (4) drug-induced hematologic or hepatic abnormality, or (5) drug-induced allergy. Severe hemorrhage was defined by the Global Utilization of Streptokinase and Tissue Plasminogen Activator for Occluded Coronary Artries (GUSTO) criteria as intracranial hemorrhage or bleeding that causes hemodynamic compromise and requires intervention.(28)  

# Follow-up  

The follow-up was conducted every 3 months. The recurrence of ischemic stroke was assessed at each follow-up. Patients were followed up by telephone survey at 3, 9, 15, and 21 months after enrollment. Office visit was conducted at 6, 12 and 18 months.At study enrollment and the 24-month follow-up, patients underwent a complete physical examination and standard analyses of blood biochemistry (hemoglobin level and counts of white blood cells, neutrophils, red blood cells, platelets, hepatic and renal function) and urine biochemistry (protein, leukocytes, erythrocytes).  

# Statistical Analysis  

Outcomes were analyzed based on the intentionto-treat principle. Safety analysis included all patients who received at least one dose of the investigational drug. Categorical variables were analyzed using chi-squared or Fisher's exact tests (when the event number $\mathtt{<10}$ per group). Continuous, normally distributed data were presented as mean $\pm$ standard deviation $(\bar{\pmbx}\pm{\pmbs})$ , and analyzed using Student's t-test. Continuous, skewed data were presented as median (interquartile range), and analyzed using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Statistical significance was defined as $P{<}0.05$ (two-sided). All statistical analyses were conducted using SAS 9.4 software (SAS Institute, USA).  

# RESULTS  

# Baseline Data  

A total of 2,200 patients were enrolled, and 1,100 in each group (Figure 1). In the Naoxintong group, 957 patients completed the trial, while 2 did not take any dose after randomization and 141 dropped out. In the placebo group, 942 patients completed the therapy course, 1 did not take any dose, and 157 dropped out. No significant differences were observed in the baseline characteristics between groups $_{\cdot\bar{P}>0.05}$ , Table 1).

# Outcomes  

# Primary Outcome  

Compared with the placebo group, the recurrence rate of ischemic stroke within 2 years was significantly lower in the Naoxintong group $[6.5\%$ vs. $9.5\%$ , hazard ratio (HR): 0.665, $95\%$ confidence interval (CI): 0.492–0.899, $_{P=0.008}$ , Figure 2, Table 2].  

# Secondary Outcomes  

The rate of myocardial infarction was $0.3\%$ in the Naoxintong group, and $0.5\%$ in the control group. In each group, 3 patients $(0.3\%)$ died because of a new occurrence of stroke. The all-cause mortality rate was similar between both groups: $3.1\%$ in the Naoxintong group and $2.2\%$ in the control group. The two groups showed no significant differences in the secondary outcomes ( $_{\cdot\bar{P}>0.05}$ , Table 2).

# DISCUSSION  

Naoxintong Capsule reduced the recurrent rate of ischemic stroke during 24-month observation compared with the placebo group $^{\prime}P{<}0.05)$ . In subgroup analysis, a lower recurrent rate was found in patients ${<}65$ years of age in the Naoxintong group $(P{<}0.01)$ . Naoxintong Capsule has been shown to improve lipid metabolism and reduce the lesions of LDL in animal models.(29,30) Based on these findings, we speculate that the lower recurrence rate in younger patients ${<}65$ years of age) in the Naoxintong group may reflect varying vascular conditions in different age groups. In particular, atherosclerotic lesions tend to be more widespread in older patients; hence the benefits in such patients may be limited.  

In the Framingham Heart Study, the 5-year cumulative rate of recurrent stroke was $24\%$ in women and $42\%$ in men.(31) Our results were consistent with the Framingham study. The rate of recurrent stroke in men was higher, and the difference of 2-year recurrent rate in men was significantly lower in the Naoxintong group $(P{<}0.05)$ . To evaluate whether sex affects the recurrence of stroke, a longer observation period may be needed to reveal the sex difference. In another trial that included 5,163 patients with available BMI data, the 90-day recurrence rate was higher in the overweight group than that in the normal weight group ( $10.8\%$ vs. $8.8\%$ ).(32) In this trial, a lower recurrent rate was found in the Naoxintong group in the subgroups analyses that included patients with normal BMI only. We speculate that patients with normal BMI may have a less risk of lipid metabolism disorder. Although no significant difference was observed in obesity, the potential regulatory effects of Naoxintong Capsule were noteworthy. It may regulate the lipid metabolism of obese individuals,(19) but more studies on the mechanisms are required. No anti-hypertensive effects of the drug have been reported to date, but in patients with good hypertension control, Naoxintong Capsule showed a tendency of reducing the recurrence of ischemic stroke $(P{<}0.05)$ . This may be partly due to its circulation improvement; nevertheless further research is needed.  

Duration of disease is another risk factor for recurrent stroke. Almost $9\%$ of patients experienced a recurrent stroke within 6 months after the initial event, and the recurrent rate was approximated $4\%$ at 1 year after onset.(33) In line with these studies, we observed a lower recurrence rate in the Naoxintong group in the subgroup analysis that included patients with a disease duration ${>}3$ months $(P{<}0.01)$ .  

A previous study showed that Naoxintong Capsule enhanced the antiplatelet effects when used as an adjunct to antiplatelet therapy in patients with cytochrome $\mathtt{p450~2C19^{*}2}$ polymorphism and decreased severe cardiovascular events.(34) Compared to warfarin alone, Naoxintong Capsule in combination with aspirin reduced 1-year rate of severe bleeding in elderly patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation.(20) These results suggested that Naoxintong Capsule may provide effective secondary prevention of stroke without increasing the severe adverse events. The rate of severe hemorrhage was $0.73\%$ in the Naoxintong group versus $1.18\%$ in the placebo group in current study $(P>0.05)$ . This finding clearly demonstrated that Naoxintong Capsule did not increase the risk of severe bleeding.  

The current trial has several limitations. Since antiplatelet therapy and anticoagulation therapy are different, more detailed observations are needed. Second, we limited the minimum onset time of ischemic stroke, without an upper limitation of the disease duration. Although the prognosis of the patient was comparable, the recurrence rate of ischemic stroke was far higher among patients with shorter disease duration than those with long duration from stroke onset. Finally, we did not monitor platelet function, $\mathsf{HbA1c\%}$ and LDL-C during the follow-up period, which may objectively explain the mechanism of Naoxintong Capsule.  

In conclusion, this trial demonstrated that Naoxintong Capsule could reduce the recurrence rate of ischemic stroke without increasing the risk of severe hemorrhage in high-risk patients.
