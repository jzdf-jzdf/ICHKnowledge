# The effect of Tianjiang Xueshuantong Wan pills on ischemia-reperfusion injury after thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction  

Rui Xian Wanga,\*, Yajuan Shaoa, Xiujuan Yanga, Xiangning Zhanga, Yue Hui Wanga, Xiaolong Lia, Jing Wanga, Feng Jieb

Acute cerebral infarction   
Venous thrombolysis   
Reperfusion injury Objective: The aim of this study is to investigate the effect of Tianjiang Xueshuantong Wan pills on reperfusion injury after venous thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction.   
Methods: The strategy used in this study is a randomised controlled clinical trial. In total, 72 cases were included, with 36 in the trial group and 36 in the control group, with a 1:1 ratio. Both groups were given standardised treatment for acute cerebral infarction. Based on the rt-PA intravenous thrombolysis, the test group took Tianjiang Xueshuantong Wan pills orally, whereas the control group solely utilised rt-PA for intravenous thrombolysis and did not take the test medicine orally.   
The patients’ intracranial hemorrhage was clarified by head CT scan, and the occurrence of reperfusion injury was recorded during the entire trial.   
Results: There were no significant differences in serum IL-6, MDA, SOD and TNF concentrations and NIHSS scores between the two groups before therapy $(P>0.05)$ . After treatment, the serum concentrations of IL-6, MDA and TNF in the experimental group were significantly decreased compared with the control group, while the serum concentrations of SOD were significantly increased compared with the control group, with statistical significance $\begin{array}{r}{\mathit{P}>0.05\$ ). After seven days of treatment, the total effective rate in the experimental group was $88.89\%$ , while the data in the control group was $75\%$ . There was a statistically significant difference between the experimental and control groups.  

Conclusion: Tianjiang Xueshuantong Wan pills can effectively prevent reperfusion injury following intravenous thrombolysis in individuals with cerebral infarction while improving patients’ neurological deficits.

# Introduction  

Cerebrovascular disease, often known as stroke, is a significant ailment that has a negative impact on individuals’ quality of life and health.1 It is now the world’s second leading cause of death and the main cause of disability.2 As a clinical syndrome, stroke has three main pathologies: ischemic stroke, cerebral hemorrhage and subarachnoid hemorrhage,3 with ischemic stroke accounting for around $80\%$ of cases and characterised by significant morbidity, recurrence, disability and mortality.4 As the most helpful treatment for acute cerebral infarction, intravenous thrombolysis can save the penumbra, dissolve the thrombus and rapidly restore blood reperfusion. However, reperfusion injury can occur during thrombolysis, causing brain tissue damage and functional impairment. As a result, the priority of thrombolysis is to prevent reperfusion injury by suppressing its essential link mechanism.5 The toxic effects of oxygen free radicals and vascular endothelial damage caused by inflammatory response are the most prominent injury mechanisms of reperfusion injury. Several studies have confirmed that Buyang Huanwu Decoction, the representative formula benefiting qi for activating blood circulation, can significantly increase the activity of superoxide dismutase (SOD), reduce the level of malondialdehyde (MDA) in brain tissue and significantly reduce the levels of tumor necrosis factor (TNF), interleukin (IL) and other inflammatory cytokines in the serum, reducing the inflammatory response after cerebral ischemia and reperfusion, and plays a role in cerebral protection and inhibiting reperfusion injury.79 In our hospital, Tianjiang Xueshuantong Wan pills (batch number: Jin Yao Zhi Zi [Lin] Z20100938), containing viper, ginseng, astragalus, salvia, hawthorn, odorifera, pinellia, calamus, etc., is a nosocomial preparation based on Buyang Huanwu Decoction (mainly containing astragalus, angelica, red peony root, earth dragon, safflower, etc.). It has been used in treating cerebrovascular diseases in clinical trials for many years. It is safe and reliable and has a definite effect. Based on the previous research results, this trial was conducted to see if Tianjiang Xueshuantong pills could prevent reperfusion injury after thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction and provide a clinical trial basis for its use in the adjuvant treatment of intravenous thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction.  

# Materials and methods  

Clinical information  

The study included 72 patients who had ischemia reperfusion following intravenous thrombolysis for acute cerebral infarction and were admitted to our hospital’s Inpatient Department of Encephalopathy between April 2020 and May 2021. The patients were placed into two groups at random, one for controls and one for experimental treatment procedures, with 40 cases in each group. When comparing the general information of the individuals, such as gender, age and weight, which were comparable, there was no statistically significant difference $(P>0.05)$ between the two groups (see Table 1). The study was authorised by the Beichen District Health Bureau’s Ethics Committee.  

Inclusion and exclusion criteria  

Inclusion criteria:  

1. According to the TOAST subtype classification criteria, all patients met the Western medicine diagnostic criteria for large artery atherosclerotic ischemic cerebrovascular disease; symptoms of brain function loss remained for more than $^{1\mathrm{~h~}}$   
2. Patients developed symptoms within $4.5\mathrm{~h~}$ , head CT or MRI examination revealed cerebral ischemic changes   
3. NIHSS scores between ${\geq}7$ and ${\le}20$   
4. Met-the TCM diagnostic criteria for stroke   
5. Aged between $\geq40$ and $\le80$ years old   
6. Those who signed the informed consent and volunteered to be tested  

Exclusion criteria:  

1. The presence of intravenous thrombolysis contraindications in acute cerebral infarction   
2. Women who were pregnant or breastfeeding   
3. Allergic reactions (those who were allergic to more than two foods or medications)   
4. Patients with known allergies to Tianjiang Xueshuantong pills, their components and rt-PA   
5. Those who participated in other drug trials within two months   
6. Patients who were unable or unlikely to complete the trial as judged by the physician  

# Treatment methods  

Referring to the Chinese Guideline for Cerebrovascular Disease Prevention and Treatment, both groups received basic medical treatment and intravenous thrombolytic therapy with rt-PA (specifications: $20\mathrm{mg}_{,}$ /bottle, $50~\mathrm{mg}/$ bottle, Boehringer Ingelheim Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.), administered at $0.9\mathrm{mg/kg;10\%}$ of the drug was administered intravenously and injected within $1\mathrm{min}$ , the remaining $90\%$ was added to $100\mathrm{ml}$ of $0.9\%$ saline and pumped within $^{1\textrm{h}}$ . Patients in the test group received intravenous thrombolysis with rt-PA followed by oral administration of Tiandian Thromboxane pills, whereas patients in the control group received only intravenous rt-PA and no oral administration of the test medication. After $^{24\mathrm{~h~}}$ all patients received Edaravone to improve cerebral metabolism, antiplatelet aggregation therapy with clopidogrel bisulphate and aspirin, and symptomatic conventional treatments, such as control of blood glucose, blood lipids, blood pressure and maintenance of electrolyte balance.  

# Observation indicators  

The changes in serum SOD, MDA, TNF and IL-6 in the two groups were assessed by the Aidikang Medical Testing center (Hangzhou, China) before and seven days after thrombolysis. The SOD, MDA, TNF and IL-6 were tested by enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay. The NIHSS scale was used to evaluate the changes of consciousness, language, movement and other neurological functions in the two groups; the intracranial hemorrhage in the patients was clarified by head CT plain scan, and the occurrence of reperfusion injury in the two groups was recorded throughout the test.  

Efficacy evaluation  

The clinical effectiveness was assessed using the 2015 International Stroke NIHSS score.10 Patients with an NIHSS score of $90\%$ or more were considered cured, $46\%$ to $89\%$ were considered significantly effective, $18\%$ to $45\%$ were considered effective and $18\%$ or less were considered ineffective. The overall response rate $=$ (basically recovered $^+$ significantly effective $^+$ effective) cases $/$ total ${\ttCases}\times100\%$ .  

# Statistical methods  

The SPSS 19.0 software was used for statistical analysis. The measurement data were expressed as mean $\pm$ standard deviation $(\overline{{x}}\pms)$ , and the count data were expressed as a percentage $(\%)$ . The $t$ -test was used to compare the measurement data with normal distribution between groups, whereas the non-normal distribution data and the count data were assessed by a non-parametric test. $P\le0.05$ was considered a statistically significant difference.  

# Results  

Comparison of serum IL-6, MDA, TNF and SOD levels before and after treatment between the two groups  

Before treatment, there were no significant differences in serum IL-6, MDA, TNF and SOD levels between the two groups. After seven days of treatment, the serum IL-6, MDA and TNF levels in the experimental group significantly decreased compared with the control group, and the serum SOD levels increased significantly compared with the control group. The differences were statistically significant $\mathrm{\mathit{~P~}}<0.05$ ). See Table 2 for more information.

# Discussion  

In recent studies, reperfusion injury after thrombolysis is linked to a variety of mechanisms, including excessive free radical formation, inflammatory response, toxic effects of excitatory amino acids and intracellular calcium overload, with the toxic effects of oxygen radicals and vascular endothelial damage caused by the inflammatory response being the most important mechanisms of injury. On the one hand, free radicals play a toxic role in the late ischemic and reperfusion periods, causing the penumbra to evolve into an infarcted area,11 as well as compromising the integrity of brain cells and the blood-brain barrier, worsening the ischemic damage.12 Furthermore, lipid peroxides such as MDA and lactate dehydrogenase are significantly elevated during ischemia, resulting in an oxidationperoxidation imbalance and the creation of a vicious cycle.13 At the same time, the obstruction of the oxygen free radical scavenging system is a major contributor to the excessive formation of free radicals. SOD inhibits the generation of free radicals and acts as an antioxidant, and its concentration reflects the human body’s ability to scavenge oxygen free radicals to a great extent.14 On the other hand, the huge number of inflammatory mediators released by the sick tissue during ischemia reperfusion activates vascular endothelial and inflammatory cells, resulting in a direct killing effect. In addition, the overexpression of adhesion molecules on the surface of inflammatory and vascular endothelial cells causes inflammatory cells to adhere to the vascular endothelium, releasing a large number of toxic oxygen radicals and protein hydrolases, increasing the permeability of endothelial cells, thus aggravating tissue damage. There are many inflammatory mediators involved in the inflammatory response in the brain, the most common of which are cytokines, including TNF and IL.15 TNF can cause damage by combining and activating other multiple pathways that cause cerebral ischemia/reperfusion injury. Overexpression of IL-6 can produce destructive effects on neuronal and vascular endothelial cells, and dysregulation of its expression is an important indicator of the extent of ischemia/reperfusion injury.16

In terms of neurological function evaluation, the test results showed that treatment in the experimental group could more effectively reduce NIHSS scores and improve neurological deficits compared with the control group, as well as improving patients’ clinical symptoms on an individual level. The test results showed that treatment in the experimental group could effectively reduce serum IL-6 and TNF levels compared with the control group, which had a protective effect on brain cells by effectively inhibiting the inflammatory response caused by reperfusion injury. In addition, treatment in the experimental group was more effective than in the control group in reducing the serum MDA level, preventing apoptosis and neurological damage by reducing lipid peroxidation caused by reperfusion injury after intravenous thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction, improving vascular endothelial function and preventing the occurrence of reperfusion injury after thrombolysis to the greatest extent. On the other hand, treatment in the experimental group could effectively prevent the reduction of oxygen radical scavenger SOD compared with the control group, which could protect the function of the oxygen radical scavenging system, thus inhibiting the generation of lipid peroxide, reducing the oxygen radical-mediated lipid peroxidation reaction, reducing the damage of free radicals on vascular endothelium and playing a cerebral protective effect.  

# Conclusion  

In conclusion, Tianjiang Xueshuantong pills can effectively prevent reperfusion injury after intravenous thrombolysis in acute cerebral infarction by reducing inflammation and lipid peroxidation while improving the function of the oxygen free radical scavenging system, as well as helping patients with neurological deficits. However, due to the small sample size and the fact that it was a singlecenter study, as well as the short observation time, this trial had limitations and failed to systematically analyze long-term efficacy, which should be improved in the follow-up study.
